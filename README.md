# FixBondhu: A Location-Based Home Repair Service Platform

FixBondhu is a location-based web platform that connects customers with nearby verified skilled service providers, such as plumbers, electricians, air conditioner technicians, and gas stove repair workers.[cite: 1] The platform addresses the common problem of finding a trustworthy technician quickly during household emergencies, such as water leaks, electrical faults, or gas stove failures.[cite: 1] By replacing the informal reliance on word-of-mouth or outdated phone numbers, FixBondhu provides a system where customers can view a worker's reliability, current availability, distance, and fair pricing.[cite: 1] 

## Unique Selling Point
The platform's unique selling point is speed combined with trust, offering the fastest way to find a verified, nearby technician during an emergency.[cite: 1]

## Key Features
* **Location-Based Discovery:** Customers can discover available workers around them on a live map, with search results ranked by distance, rating, and availability.[cite: 1]
* **Verified Trust System:** Workers create profiles with their skills and service categories, and the system grants a verified badge upon admin approval of their National ID (NID) and skill evidence.[cite: 1]
* **Emergency Booking Flow:** Customers can create normal or emergency service requests, attach photos of the issue, and view upfront price and time estimates before booking.[cite: 1]
* **Live Request Tracking:** Customers and technicians can track a request through its full lifecycle, from the moment it is sent to its completion.[cite: 1]
* **Ratings and Moderation:** Customers can rate and review a worker from one to five stars after a completed service, while administrators handle dispute resolution, moderation, and fraud prevention.[cite: 1]

## System Architecture
The platform is built on a three-tier architecture to separate presentation, application logic, and data:[cite: 1]
* **Presentation Tier (Web App):** Manages the interfaces for customers, technicians, and administrators, covering the map, profiles, search, and tracking dashboards.[cite: 1]
* **Application Tier (Backend / API):** Handles the core business logic, including account verification, location-based matching, status transitions, and role-based access control.[cite: 1]
* **Data Tier (Database):** Provides persistent storage for users, worker profiles, service requests, reviews, service categories, and geographic coverage areas.[cite: 1]

## Future Scope
* Implementation of in-app online payments and digital invoices to enable cashless transactions.[cite: 1]
* Development of native mobile applications for iOS and Android platforms to better support emergency, on-the-go usage.[cite: 1]
* Introduction of a smart recommendation feature to automatically suggest the best-matched worker based on distance, rating, and availability.[cite: 1]
